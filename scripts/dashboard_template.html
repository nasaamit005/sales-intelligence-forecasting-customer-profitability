Key findings

    Recommended actions

      Method and limitations

      • Data: fictional Global Superstore sample (51,290 order lines). Dates are day-first in two formats; 659 order IDs are reused, so an order = order ID + date + customer.
      • Profit is the source’s reported field, not audited net profit; shipping cost is not subtracted.
      • Forecasting: 208 complete weeks; 7 models; 3 expanding-window validation folds choose the model; the last 13 weeks (Oct–Dec 2014) are held out. The held-out block was seen during development, so treat its scores as indicative. On it, simple baselines scored about 15% WAPE versus 18% for the selected model.
      • Bug found and fixed: an earlier level-based XGBoost forecast came out at 1.70x last year’s Q1; the year-over-year-ratio model and a plausibility test replaced it.
      • Intervals are empirical (10th–90th percentile of actual/forecast ratios in validation), not model-based.
      • Not claimed: causal discount effects, forward-looking CLV, inventory optimisation, returns analysis.
